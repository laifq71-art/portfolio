# Spec Work — 30-Day Content Plan for a DeFi Protocol Launch

*What this is: a sample content strategy showing how I'd run social + content for a new DeFi protocol going to mainnet. Written as spec work to demonstrate strategic thinking, not just execution.*

---

## Context (assumed)

Fictional lending protocol "Tide" launching on Solana. Audited, $2M seed, no token yet. Goal: 10k waitlist signups + 3k active testnet users in 30 days.

## Content Pillars

1. **Explain** — make lending mechanics obvious to non-DeFi natives (60% of output)
2. **Prove** — audits, testnet stats, team credibility (20%)
3. **Belong** — memes, community rituals, inside jokes (20%)

## Week-by-Week Plan

### Week 1 — "Why Tide exists"
- Launch thread: the problem with current lending UX (8 posts, story-driven)
- 2 infographics: how Tide's rates work vs. incumbents
- Founder intro video (60s, captioned)
- KPI: 2k followers, 500 waitlist

### Week 2 — "Show, don't tell"
- Testnet walkthrough thread with screenshots
- "DeFi lending in 60 seconds" short video
- First community meme contest (small USDC prizes)
- KPI: 1.5k testnet users, 3k followers

### Week 3 — "Bring receipts"
- Audit summary thread (what was checked, what was fixed — transparency wins)
- Testnet stats infographic: users, volume, bugs squashed
- 2 quote-tweets of community wins/testimonials
- KPI: 3k testnet users, 5k followers

### Week 4 — "Mainnet countdown"
- 5-day countdown series: one feature per day
- Launch-day thread template (pre-written, timed)
- Post-launch: "first 24h stats" recap within 12 hours
- KPI: 10k waitlist, mainnet trending on Crypto Twitter

## Channel Mix

| Channel | Role | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| X/Twitter | Primary. Threads, infographics, memes | 2–3 posts/day |
| Discord | Community hub. AMAs, support, rituals | Weekly AMA |
| Blog/Mirror | SEO + depth. 1 long-form/week | Weekly |
| TikTok/Reels | Top-of-funnel explainers | 2/week |

## What I'd Measure

- North star: testnet→mainnet conversion rate
- Leading: thread impressions, profile visits, waitlist adds
- Lagging: TVL at day 7/30, retention of week-1 users
- Kill fast: any format under 0.5% engagement after 3 tries gets cut

## Why This Works

Most protocol launches post *announcements*. This plan posts *a story in four acts* — problem, proof, credibility, climax — with the community as characters, not audience. People don't share press releases. They share things that made them feel early.
