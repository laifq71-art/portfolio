**5/** The wallets that DO make money share 4 traits:
- Realized PnL positive (not paper gains)
- 50+ settled bets (not a lucky streak)
- Months of history (not a hot week)
- Human pace (not HFT you can't copy)

**6/** The meta-lesson: in prediction markets, everyone sells you the *signal*.

99% of "opportunities" die at step 2: can I actually execute this?
